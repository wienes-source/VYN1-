## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end Dutch interim management and staffing consultancy ("Connecting Talent. Creating Value."). It combines institutional trust, premium executive credibility, and modern dynamism.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** clarity with **Contemporary Precision Glass & Tonal Layers**:
- **Target Audience:** C-suite directors, HR leaders, procurement partners, and elite interim specialists across finance, tech, operations, and management.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, forward momentum, precision, dependability, and top-tier professionalism.
- **Visual Tenets:** Pure canvas whites, micro-subtle slate borders, lush botanical greens paired with vivid electric lime highlights (drawn from the ribbon-folded logo), and disciplined typographic balance.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the dimensional ribbon styling of the logo mark:
- **Primary (`#16a34a`):** Crisp, trustworthy emerald green used for primary interactive states, key callouts, and brand focal anchors.
- **Secondary (`#064e3b`):** Deep prestige forest green representing structural stability, header navigation accents, dark contrast containers, and authority.
- **Tertiary (`#22c55e`):** Vibrant lime-emerald accent for active pill indicators, gradient endpoints, hover glows, and status badges.
- **Neutral (`#0f172a`):** Deep slate-navy for authoritative typography and high-contrast headlines, accompanied by `#334155` for body text, `#f8fafc` for subtle tinted background sections, and `#e2e8f0` for crisp container borders.

Gradients should mirror the ribbon folding from deep forest (`#064e3b`) into vivid lime (`#22c55e`) sparingly across hero banners, primary action buttons, and metric callouts.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a contemporary European corporate feel: crisp geometric legibility paired with open apertures that evoke approachability and modern professionalism.

- Use `subtitle-tracked` in uppercase for overline categories (e.g., "INTERIM & DETACHERING") reflecting the geometric tracking of the logo subline.
- Display sizes command attention with tight kerning (`-0.03em`) and substantial weight (`700`-`800`).
- Ensure high contrast against light surfaces with neutral `#0f172a` for headlines and `#334155` for narrative body copy.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system drives desktop layouts (max container width 1280px), transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** Generous 48px (`margin: 3rem`) canvas margins and 24px gutters to evoke open breathing room and executive authority.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 32px canvas margins and 20px gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Compact 20px (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`) padding and 16px gutters.
- Vertical section rhythm follows 80px to 112px intervals on desktop for clear visual breathing room between case studies, talent specializations, and testimonials.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through refined surface separation rather than heavy skeuomorphism:

1. **Base Flat Surfaces:** Background fills alternate between `#ffffff` and subtle slate-ice `#f8fafc` bounded by hairline `#e2e8f0` borders.
2. **Elevated Cards:** Hover states and floating cards utilize a triple-layer ambient shadow with a hint of deep forest tint: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04), 0 10px 25px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
3. **Hero & Accent Floating Modules:** Translucent glassmorphism (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a semi-transparent border (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.2)`).
4. **Active/CTA Glow:** Primary interaction targets cast a delicate emerald radiance (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an executive pill-oriented geometry (`roundedness: 3`):
- **Buttons, status badges, and search chips** adopt full pill radii (`9999px` / `rounded-full`).
- **Feature cards, profile modules, and input wrappers** use generous rounded corners (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / `rounded-2xl`) with crisp 1px borders.
- **Ribbon-inspired graphical accents** introduce organic geometric curves that mirror the folded "VYN1" logo identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), dynamic horizontal gradient from `#16a34a` to `#22c55e`, white text (`#ffffff`), `font-weight: 600`, subtle emerald drop shadow. On hover, smooth vertical lift (-2px) and heightened glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Deep forest green border (`#064e3b`, 1.5px), transparent background, `#064e3b` text, transitioning on hover to a `#f0fdf4` soft fill.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Slate text (`#334155`), transparent background, slight background tint (`#f1f5f9`) on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Sector Badges:** Pill-shaped, extra-small tracking. For example, "Interim Professional": pale green tint background (`#dcfce7`), deep emerald text (`#166534`), with an optional 6px pulsing emerald dot indicator.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with 1px slate-200 outline, white fill, transitioning to solid deep forest green with white text when selected.

### Cards
- **Talent & Assignment Cards:** White base surface, 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem), 24px internal padding. Subtle hover translation with ambient green-tinted shadow and border color shift to `#86efac`.
- **Dark Metric Cards:** Solid deep forest `#064e3b` surface with crisp lime accents (`#22c55e`) for placement statistics and performance metrics.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Select Fields:** 48px height, 12px horizontal radius or full pill depending on contextual prominence, 1px border (`#cbd5e1`), crisp `#0f172a` typography with `#94a3b8` placeholder.
- **Focus State:** 2px focus ring in `#16a34a` with soft 4px outline blur (`rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.2)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px rounded squares (4px radius), 1.5px border (`#cbd5e1`). Checked state fills with `#16a34a` featuring a crisp white check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circular indicators; outer ring `#16a34a` with solid center bullet when active.

### Specialized Interim Modules
- **Talent Match Matrix:** Split-pane interface showcasing availability badges, core competencies (pill chips), and direct executive contact hooks.
